Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Ludo Dost ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user of the Ludo Dost mobile application (com.ludodost.app).

1. Information We Collect

Ludo Dost does not require account creation or sign-in. We collect only the minimum data needed:

  • Device information (type, OS version, app version) for bug fixing and compatibility.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google AdMob may collect your Android Advertising ID. See Section 3.
  • Gameplay data (on-device only): progress and scores stored locally, not transmitted to our servers.

We do NOT collect your name, email, phone number, location, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the Ludo Dost application.
  • To display advertisements via Google AdMob (see Section 3).
  • To diagnose and fix technical issues.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Ludo Dost shows advertisements served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect your Android Advertising ID and device data to deliver ads. To opt out: Settings > Privacy > Ads > Opt out of Ads Personalisation. See Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. The only third party receiving data is Google AdMob for ad delivery, governed by Google's privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

Game progress is stored locally on your device only. Uninstalling the app deletes all local data. We do not store personal data on our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Ludo Dost is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal data and we will delete it promptly.

7. Security

We collect minimal data and do not operate user accounts or a personal data backend. Gameplay data on your device is protected by your device's own security mechanisms.

8. Your Rights

  • Reset your Android Advertising ID in device settings.
  • Opt out of personalised advertising in device settings.
  • Delete all local app data by uninstalling the app.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with a revised date. Continued use of Ludo Dost constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Compliance

This app complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDP Act) and Google Play's Developer Program Policies.
